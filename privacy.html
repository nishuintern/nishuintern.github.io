Privacy Policy

Curious Stick Studio · "Curious Stick Uploader" · Effective 25 October 2026

Who uses the app

Curious Stick Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of Curious Stick Studio to manage the studio's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission, through Google's official YouTube Data API, the app uploads videos, sets their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and schedules, and changes the visibility of videos on the owner's own channels. It reads only the information needed for that, such as the channel identity and video status.

What we collect

We do not collect, sell or share personal data about viewers or anyone else. Google sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and server, are used only to perform the actions above, and are never shared with third parties.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
